## Hands On
Name | Description | Account?
---- | ----------- | --------
[pwnable.tw](http://pwnable.tw/) | hosts 27 challenges accompanied with writeups, account required | Free
[pwnable.kr](http://pwnable.kr/) | Don't let the cartoon characters fool you, this is a serious CTF site that will teach you a lot, account required | Free
[reversing.kr challenges](http://www.reversing.kr/challenge.php) | reverse engineering challenges varying in difficulty | Free
[microcorruption.com](https://microcorruption.com/login) | Embedded Security CTF | No
[CrackMEs.one](https://crackmes.one/) | A list of a lot of crackmes for reverse engineering | No
[ROP Emporium](https://ropemporium.com/) | Return Oriented Programming challenges | No
[Challenges RE](https://challenges.re/) | A list of challengegs by Dennis Yurichev | No
[Modern Binary Exploitation - CSCI 4968](https://github.com/RPISEC/MBE) | RE challenges, you can download the files or download the VM created by RPISEC specifically for challenges, also links to their home page with tons of infosec lectures | No
[Tuts 4 You](https://forum.tuts4you.com/files/categories/) | A collection of blog posts about reverse enggineering at all levels, and also a collection of RE challenges | Free
[Lena's Reversing for Newbies (Complete)](https://tuts4you.com/download.php?view.2876) | listing of a lengthy resource by Lena, aimed at being a course (hosted on the site above) | Free
